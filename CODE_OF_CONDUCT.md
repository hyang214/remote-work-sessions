# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Keep public discussions focused on the project and its community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a
participant. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and whether
the behavior is repeated.

Report conduct concerns to a maintainer using a contact method published on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not put another person's private information in
a public issue. Maintainers will keep the reporter's identity confidential when
reasonably possible and will not participate in resolving a report in which
they have a conflict of interest.
